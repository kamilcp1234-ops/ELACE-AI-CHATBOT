# SLA/MSLA 3D Printing at ELACE

## SLA/MSLA equipment

ELACE reports resin-printing equipment including:
- Elegoo Mars series
- Elegoo Saturn
- 2 × Elegoo Saturn Ultra
- Anycubic Jupiter Mono
- Elegoo Mars 5 Ultra

The exact active machine count should be maintained in the internal equipment inventory if it changes.

## Mars 5 Ultra build volume

The Elegoo Mars 5 Ultra has a manufacturer-listed build volume of:

**153.36 × 77.76 × 165 mm**

The manufacturer also lists a Z-axis accuracy of **0.02 mm** and a layer-thickness range of **0.01–0.2 mm**.

ELACE uses the Mars 5 Ultra's 0.02 mm Z-axis accuracy as the initial reference for the dimensional-accuracy claim for parts printed on this platform. This should be understood as the printer manufacturer's stated Z-axis accuracy, not as a universal guarantee that every finished part will measure within ±0.02 mm.

## Cleaning and curing

ELACE has:
- a dedicated alcohol tank for cleaning SLA prints,
- a dedicated curing station for post-curing.

## Resin types

Commonly available resin categories include:
- standard/regular resin,
- ABS-like resin,
- transparent resin.

Specific resins can be sourced based on customer requirements.

Typical sourcing time for a requested resin is approximately 3–4 days when normally available. Rare or difficult-to-source resin may require approximately 1–2 weeks depending on availability.

## Layer height

Typical ELACE SLA layer height: approximately 0.02 mm.

The Mars 5 Ultra manufacturer specifies a layer-thickness range of 0.01–0.2 mm.

## Practical build size

ELACE's practical SLA print size depends on the available machine and geometry. The Mars 5 Ultra build volume is 153.36 × 77.76 × 165 mm.

ELACE previously described approximately 12 cm as a practical maximum for its SLA workflow; customers requiring a larger resin part should have the exact geometry assessed before quotation.

## Surface finish

SLA is selected when customers need:
- smooth surfaces,
- fine features,
- detailed models,
- visually refined parts.

Post-processing such as sanding and painting can also be provided.

## Typical applications

SLA is particularly suitable for:
- detailed artistic models,
- miniatures,
- feature-rich visual models,
- small detailed prototypes,
- applications where smooth surface quality is important.

FDM is generally preferred for larger or more functional parts, depending on requirements.
