# Supports and Overhangs

## Why supports are used

Supports may be required when model geometry cannot be printed reliably without temporary supporting structures.

## FDM

Supports can affect:
- print time,
- material consumption,
- surface finish,
- post-processing.

Orientation can often reduce the amount of support required.

## SLA

SLA models commonly require support structures for appropriate printing and orientation. Support placement also affects the surface finish of supported regions.

## Customer recommendation

If support marks or a specific visual surface must be minimized, communicate this requirement before production.
