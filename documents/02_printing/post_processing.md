# Post-Processing at ELACE

## Available finishing

ELACE reports capabilities including:
- sanding,
- smoothing,
- matte painting,
- glossy painting,
- artistic painting.

## Artistic painting

Artistic painting can be provided for suitable models, including models requiring human-like features or detailed artistic treatment.

## Finishing requirements

Post-processing requirements should be communicated during quotation because finishing can affect:
- cost,
- production time,
- appearance,
- dimensional fit.

## Important limitation

Not every model can receive every finishing method. Final feasibility depends on material, geometry and desired appearance.
