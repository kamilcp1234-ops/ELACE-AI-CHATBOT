# Tolerances and Clearances

## FDM

ELACE's stated typical dimensional tolerance is approximately ±0.2 mm.

This is a general planning figure, not a guarantee for every dimension or geometry.

## SLA

A layer height of approximately 0.02 mm is reported for SLA. This is not automatically equivalent to a guaranteed dimensional tolerance.

VERIFY: Confirm the actual customer-facing SLA accuracy/tolerance specification.

## Fits

If two printed components must mate, customers should specify whether the desired fit is:
- loose,
- sliding,
- press-fit,
- snap-fit,
- or another defined fit.

The required clearance depends on the process, material, geometry and fit type.

## Recommendation

For critical fits, prototype the mating geometry before committing to a large production quantity.
