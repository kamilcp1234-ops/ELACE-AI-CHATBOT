# FDM 3D Printing at ELACE

## Printer fleet

ELACE currently reports the following FDM equipment:
- 5 × Neptune 4 Max
- 1 × Bambu Lab A1
- 2 × Bambu Lab A1 Mini
- 1 × Bambu Lab H2S with AMS
- Additional Bambu Lab A1/H2S AMS capability for multi-colour printing
- 1 × Anycubic Kobra 3

## Nozzle

Typical nozzle size: 0.4 mm.

## Layer height

Typical layer height: 0.2 mm.

ELACE can accommodate different layer-height requirements, with a stated upper practical value of approximately 0.8 mm depending on the application and machine/settings.

Lower layer heights generally increase print time. Higher layer heights generally reduce print time but can reduce surface/detail quality.

## Dimensional tolerance

Typical stated tolerance: approximately ±0.2 mm.

Actual dimensional accuracy can vary with geometry, material, orientation, machine, calibration and print settings. The stated tolerance should not be interpreted as a guaranteed tolerance for every feature.

## Build size

For a single print, ELACE reports a maximum practical size of approximately 400 × 400 × 400 mm.

For Bambu Lab equipment, ELACE reports approximately 320 × 325 × 340 mm as a practical maximum.

For models larger than a single build volume, ELACE can split the model into multiple sections and join/assemble them.

ELACE has produced a model approximately eight feet in overall size using this approach.

## File formats

ELACE accepts:
- STEP files (preferred)
- STL files
- OBJ files
- Blender files where practical

When submitting an STL file, the customer should specify the exact intended dimensions because STL scaling can sometimes result in an unintended model size.

## Materials

ELACE reports availability of a broad range of filament types, including:
- PLA
- PETG
- ABS
- ASA
- TPE
- TPU
- flexible TPU grades such as 95A and 87A
- specialty filaments such as silk/gold-type PLA
- other materials subject to availability

Customers may request a specific filament manufacturer/brand. ELACE reports using or sourcing brands including:
- eSUN
- Elegoo
- Bambu Lab
- World 3D
- Newmakers

Specific brand/material availability should be confirmed before quotation.

## Colours

Common ready-stock colours include:
- white
- black
- grey
- blue

Other colours may be available based on stock or can be sourced.

Specific shades such as lagoon, royal blue, navy blue or other uncommon colours may require additional procurement time.

Typical procurement time for a specific colour/filament is approximately 3 days to 1 week depending on availability.

## Multi-colour printing

ELACE reports AMS capability on applicable Bambu Lab equipment and can provide multi-colour printing where the machine, model and material requirements support it.

## Pricing

Print pricing depends on factors including print time, material, layer height, model requirements and order quantity.

Bulk orders may be eligible for negotiated pricing.

## CAD and model preparation

ELACE can provide CAD/3D design services and can attempt to repair customer-supplied models.

## Post-processing

FDM prints can receive sanding/smoothing and painting. Glossy, matte and artistic finishes may be available depending on the model.
