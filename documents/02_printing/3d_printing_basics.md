# 3D Printing Basics

## What is 3D printing?

3D printing is an additive manufacturing process in which a physical object is produced layer by layer from digital geometry.

## Choosing between FDM and SLA

### FDM

FDM is generally suitable when customers need:
- functional parts,
- larger parts,
- practical engineering components,
- a broad selection of thermoplastic materials,
- flexible materials,
- economical prototypes.

### SLA

SLA is generally suitable when customers need:
- smooth surfaces,
- fine details,
- small features,
- detailed artistic or visual models,
- high-detail prototypes.

## Important limitation

The best process depends on the part's geometry, required strength, dimensional requirements, surface finish, material requirements and intended use.

Customers should provide the application and requirements when requesting a quotation so ELACE can recommend a suitable process.
