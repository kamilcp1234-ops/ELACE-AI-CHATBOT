# Custom 3D Printing

ELACE accepts custom printing requirements for individuals, students and businesses.

## Customer can provide

- an existing 3D model,
- an image,
- a 2D reference,
- or an idea.

## ELACE can provide

- CAD/3D design,
- model creation,
- model repair where practical,
- FDM printing,
- SLA printing,
- finishing,
- painting.

## Quotation

Custom projects are quoted based on the actual requirement.

## Production

A 50% advance is required before production starts.

## Shipping

For custom orders, shipping charges are handled by the customer.
