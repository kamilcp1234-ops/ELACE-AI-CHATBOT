# CAD and 3D Design Services

## Design from an idea

Customers can approach ELACE with an idea or image and request 3D/CAD development.

## 2D-to-3D

ELACE can create a 3D model from suitable 2D references.

## Customer model repair

ELACE can attempt to repair model geometry where practical.

## Pricing

The standard design charge is approximately ₹500–₹600 per hour depending on complexity.

For students, ELACE generally waives design charges for simple designs/small requirements and may provide a discount.

## Important

Final design cost depends on complexity and scope. The chatbot should not present ₹500–₹600/hour as a guaranteed quotation for every project.
