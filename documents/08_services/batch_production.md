# Batch and Bulk Production

ELACE can support bulk/batch requirements.

## Pricing

For larger order quantities, pricing may be negotiated based on volume and project requirements.

## Assessment

Batch-production quotations may consider:
- quantity,
- material,
- cycle time,
- post-processing,
- assembly,
- quality requirements,
- packaging,
- delivery requirements.

Customers should contact ELACE for a project-specific quotation.
