# Post-Processing and Painting Services

ELACE can provide:
- sanding,
- smoothing,
- matte finish,
- glossy finish,
- artistic painting.

Artistic painting can be performed for suitable models requiring detailed human-like features or other artistic treatment.

Finishing cost and feasibility depend on the model and requested finish.
