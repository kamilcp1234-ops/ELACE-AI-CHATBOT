# Prototyping Services

ELACE provides prototyping support for:
- students,
- individuals,
- businesses,
- industrial customers.

The appropriate process may be FDM or SLA depending on:
- required strength,
- surface finish,
- feature size,
- dimensions,
- intended use,
- material requirements.

Customers should provide the intended application and model/idea for assessment.
