# Shipment Tracking

ELACE may ship through delivery services such as Rapido or Porter.

VERIFY: A live tracking integration and official tracking workflow were not supplied.

The chatbot must not invent a tracking number or claim that an order has been dispatched unless that information is available from an authorized order/shipping system.
