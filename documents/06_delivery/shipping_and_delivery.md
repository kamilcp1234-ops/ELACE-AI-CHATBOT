# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery partners

ELACE may use services such as:
- Rapido,
- Porter,
- or another delivery service requested/selected by the customer, where feasible.

## Shipping charges

For custom orders, shipping charges are handled by the customer.

## Shipment timing

The order is shipped after:
1. production is complete,
2. completed-item images have been sent,
3. the remaining 50% payment has been received.

## Delivery estimate

Delivery timing depends on the delivery service, destination, order size and production readiness.
