# Production Lead Times

## Small parts

For small parts in approximately the 500–600 g range, ELACE reports that printing and shipping can sometimes be completed in approximately 1–2 days, subject to printer availability and production readiness.

## Very small parts

For very small parts around 100–200 g, same-day shipment may sometimes be possible when the order is placed in the morning, subject to printer availability, production readiness and material availability.

## Urgent orders

ELACE accepts urgent orders when they are within its scope and operational capacity.

Urgent delivery should never be promised unless ELACE can realistically meet the requested deadline.

## Larger or complex orders

Lead time depends on:
- print size,
- quantity,
- print time,
- material availability,
- printer availability,
- design/CAD work,
- post-processing,
- assembly,
- shipping.

The chatbot should avoid giving a guaranteed delivery date unless the order has been specifically assessed.
