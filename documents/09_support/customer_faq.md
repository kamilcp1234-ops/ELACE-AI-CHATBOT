# Customer FAQ

## How much does 3D printing cost?

There is no single universal price. ELACE quotes based on factors such as material, print time, layer height, model complexity, quantity and finishing.

## How fast can you print?

Small parts around 100–200 g may sometimes be shipped the same day when ordered in the morning, subject to printer/material availability and production readiness. Small parts around 500–600 g may sometimes be printed and shipped in 1–2 days.

## Do you offer urgent orders?

Yes, where the requirement is within ELACE's operational scope. Urgent delivery should be confirmed before it is promised.

## Can I provide my own model?

Yes. ELACE can work with customer-supplied models, subject to printability.

## Can you design the model for me?

Yes. ELACE provides CAD/3D design assistance and can create models from ideas/images/2D references.

## Do you print flexible materials?

Yes. ELACE reports TPU and TPE capability, including TPU grades such as 95A and 87A.

## Can you print a specific brand?

ELACE can use or source specific brands where available.

## Do you provide painting?

Yes. Sanding, smoothing, matte, glossy and artistic painting are available depending on the model.

## Do you accept returns?

Generally no, unless the item is damaged/defective due to an ELACE-side issue. See the return and replacement policies.

## Do you provide warranty?

No general warranty is provided unless a warranty is specifically mentioned for an individual order by the sales representative.
