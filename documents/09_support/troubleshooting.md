# 3D Printing Troubleshooting

## My print failed

First determine whether:
- the model was supplied by the customer,
- ELACE designed the model,
- the failure occurred during printing,
- the model has geometry issues,
- material availability or machine conditions affected the print.

Customer-design failures are subject to the customer-design responsibility policy.

If ELACE designed the model and the failure is due to an ELACE-side design/dimensional issue, the replacement policy may apply.

## My part dimensions are wrong

For FDM, ELACE's stated typical tolerance is approximately ±0.2 mm. Actual results depend on geometry, material and process.

If ELACE designed the model and the result has an ELACE-side dimensional accuracy issue, the replacement policy may apply.

## I need a different colour

ELACE can provide many colours subject to stock. Specific shades may require sourcing, typically around 3 days to 1 week depending on availability.

## I need a particular material

ELACE can source requested filament/resin where available. Typical sourcing time is approximately 3–4 days for commonly obtainable materials and longer for rare materials.

## I need the order urgently

Urgent orders may be possible within ELACE's operational scope. Do not assume or promise a deadline until ELACE confirms feasibility.
