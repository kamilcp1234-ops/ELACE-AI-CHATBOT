# When the Chatbot Should Escalate to ELACE Staff

The chatbot should recommend direct contact when:

- the customer requests a policy exception;
- the customer requests a refund amount that is not explicitly defined;
- the customer disputes a defect assessment;
- responsibility for a failed print is unclear;
- the customer needs a specific engineering/material guarantee;
- the requested material is not documented;
- the customer requests a live quotation;
- the customer asks about live order status;
- the customer needs live shipment tracking;
- the requested product is not documented;
- a confidential/NDA project is involved;
- the customer requests an exact delivery guarantee;
- the information in the knowledge base is missing or contradictory.
