# Order Cancellation Policy

## Cancellation threshold

Cancellation is subject to the progress of printing.

### Less than 40% print completion

If printing is below 40% completion, ELACE may provide only part of the advance amount as a refund.

The exact refund amount is not defined in the chatbot knowledge base.

### More than 40% print completion

Once printing is more than 40% complete, cancellation is not accepted.

## Human escalation

Customers requesting cancellation or a refund should speak with an ELACE human representative for the exact applicable refund amount.

The chatbot must not invent a refund percentage or calculation.
