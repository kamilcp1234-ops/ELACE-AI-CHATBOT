# Print Defect and Design Responsibility Policy

## Customer-provided design

If a customer-provided design contains faults and the resulting print fails because of those faults, ELACE does not take responsibility for the failure.

## ELACE-designed model

If ELACE designs the model and the print fails because of an ELACE-side design or dimensional-accuracy issue, ELACE will replace the affected part free of charge.

## Assessment

When responsibility is unclear, the case should be reviewed by ELACE staff rather than resolved by the chatbot.
