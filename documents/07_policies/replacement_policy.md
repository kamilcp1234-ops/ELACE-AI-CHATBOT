# Replacement Policy

## ELACE-designed and printed parts

If ELACE designed the model and the print fails due to an ELACE-side dimensional accuracy issue or equivalent confirmed production fault, ELACE will replace the affected part free of charge.

## Customer-designed parts

If the customer's design contains faults and the print fails because of those faults, ELACE does not take responsibility for the failure under the stated policy.

## Verification

The final determination of whether a failure is caused by the design or ELACE's production/design work may require review by ELACE staff.
