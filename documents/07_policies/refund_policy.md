# Refund Policy

## General rule

ELACE does not generally accept returns unless the issue is attributable to damage or defect from ELACE's side.

## Cancellation-related refund

For order cancellation before printing is more than 40% complete, ELACE may provide only part of the advance amount as a refund, subject to the cancellation policy.

Once printing is more than 40% complete, cancellation is not accepted.

## Refund amount

The exact partial-refund percentage or calculation below the 40% print-completion threshold is not specified in the chatbot knowledge base.

For a cancellation/refund calculation, the customer should speak with an ELACE human representative.

## Important

The chatbot must not invent a refund percentage, calculation, processing timeline or exception.
