# Return Policy

## General rule

ELACE does not accept returns unless the item was damaged or defective due to an issue attributable to ELACE.

## Customer-design issues

If a customer-provided design has faults and the print fails because of those design faults, ELACE does not accept responsibility under the stated policy.

## ELACE design/print issues

If ELACE designed the model and the printed result has a dimensional-accuracy issue or fails due to an ELACE-side issue, ELACE will replace the affected part free of charge under the stated replacement policy.

## Custom orders

Custom-printed items are subject to the above policy.

## Important

VERIFY: The supplied information does not specify a formal return window, inspection process, or return shipping procedure. Do not invent these terms.
