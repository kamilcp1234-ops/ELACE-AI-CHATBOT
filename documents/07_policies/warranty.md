# Warranty Policy

## General rule

ELACE does not provide a general warranty unless a warranty is specifically mentioned by the sales representative for an individual order.

## Order-specific warranty

If a sales representative explicitly provides a warranty for a specific order, that order-specific commitment governs the applicable warranty terms.

## Important

The chatbot must not imply that every ELACE product or print automatically carries a warranty.
