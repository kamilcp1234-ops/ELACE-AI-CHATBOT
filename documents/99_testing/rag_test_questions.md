# Initial RAG Test Questions

Use these questions to test retrieval and answer grounding after ingestion.

## Company

1. What does ELACE 3D Labs do?
2. What is ELACE's motto?
3. Do you work with students?
4. Do you provide CAD design?
5. Do you work with confidential company designs?
6. What are your business hours?

## FDM

7. What FDM printers do you have?
8. What is your typical FDM layer height?
9. What nozzle size do you normally use?
10. What is the typical FDM tolerance?
11. What is your largest single-print size?
12. Can you print multi-colour parts?
13. Can you print TPU?
14. Can I request a particular filament brand?

## SLA

15. What SLA printers do you have?
16. What is your typical SLA layer height?
17. What resins do you offer?
18. When should I choose SLA instead of FDM?
19. What is your practical SLA print size?

## Materials

20. Which material should I use for a functional part?
21. Do you have ABS?
22. Do you have ASA?
23. Do you have transparent resin?
24. Can you source a specific resin?

## Products

25. How much is the Wukong model?
26. What are the dimensions of the Wukong model?
27. What printing process is used for the Wukong model?

## Ordering

28. How do I place an order?
29. How much advance payment is required?
30. When does production start?
31. When do I pay the remaining amount?
32. Can you repair my STL?
33. What happens if my own design has a fault?

## Delivery

34. How quickly can you ship a 100 g part?
35. How quickly can you ship a 500 g part?
36. Who pays shipping charges?
37. Which delivery services do you use?

## Policies

38. Can I return a custom print?
39. What happens if ELACE's design has an error?
40. Can I cancel after production starts?
41. What happens if printing is more than 40% complete?
42. Do you provide warranty?

## Grounding tests

43. What is the exact tensile strength of your PLA?
44. What is the exact heat-deflection temperature of your ABS?
45. What is the guaranteed SLA dimensional tolerance?
46. What is today's stock of blue PLA?
47. Where is my order right now?
48. Which file format do you prefer?
49. Can I send an STL?
50. What dimensions should I specify with an STL?
51. What payment methods do you accept?
52. What is the Wukong model price?
53. What products are in your catalogue?

Questions 43–47 should NOT receive invented factual answers. They are intended to test whether the chatbot correctly recognizes missing/live information and escalates.
