# Order Status

## Production sequence

After the 50% advance is received, production begins.

After printing/production is completed, ELACE sends images of the completed item.

The remaining 50% payment is required before shipment.

## Live status

The chatbot must not claim a customer's live production or payment status unless connected to an authorized order-management system.

For a customer-specific status request, the chatbot should direct the customer to ELACE support or an approved live order-status system.
