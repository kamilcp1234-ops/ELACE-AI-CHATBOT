# Quotation Process

## How quotations work

Customers provide their requirement and ELACE prepares a quotation based on the project.

Quotation factors may include:
- material,
- print process,
- print time,
- model size,
- layer height,
- quantity,
- design/CAD work,
- post-processing,
- material sourcing,
- other project-specific requirements.

## Student pricing

Simple student design work may have design charges waived. Student discounts may also be available.

## Bulk pricing

For companies and other customers, pricing is generally more structured. Bulk orders may allow negotiation based on order quantity.

## Important

A quotation should be treated as the applicable commercial price for the specific order. The chatbot should not invent a price from generic material or printer information.
