# How to Order from ELACE 3D Labs

## Standard order flow

1. Customer provides the requirement.
2. ELACE reviews the requirement.
3. ELACE provides a quotation.
4. Customer pays an advance of 50% of the total quoted cost.
5. Production starts after the 50% advance is received.
6. ELACE completes printing/production.
7. ELACE sends images of the completed item to the customer.
8. Customer pays the remaining 50%.
9. ELACE ships the item to the customer's address.

## Important

Production does not start until the required 50% advance has been received.

## Custom requirements

Customers should provide the model/file and explain:
- intended application,
- material requirement,
- colour,
- size,
- quantity,
- finish,
- dimensional/fit requirements,
- deadline,
where applicable.

## Bulk orders

Bulk orders may be eligible for negotiated pricing depending on quantity and requirements.
