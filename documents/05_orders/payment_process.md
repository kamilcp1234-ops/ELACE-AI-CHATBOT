# Payment Process

## Accepted payment methods

ELACE currently accepts:
- NEFT/bank transfer
- UPI
- Card payments

Additional card/payment options may be added in the future.

## Advance

A 50% advance of the total quoted cost is required before production starts.

## Final payment

After printing/production is completed, ELACE sends images of the completed item to the customer.

The remaining 50% is due before shipment.

## Payment sequence

1. Quotation is provided.
2. Customer pays 50% advance.
3. Production starts.
4. Completed-item images are sent.
5. Customer pays the remaining 50%.
6. Item is shipped.
