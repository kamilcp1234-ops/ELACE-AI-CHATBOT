# PLA

## Availability

ELACE reports PLA as a regularly available FDM filament.

## Typical uses

PLA can be considered for:
- prototypes,
- visual models,
- general-purpose prints,
- decorative components,
- student projects.

## Colours

Many colours may be available. Common ready-stock colours reported by ELACE include white, black, grey and blue. Specific shades may require additional sourcing.

## Brands

ELACE can work with requested filament brands subject to availability, including brands it commonly sources.

## Important

Exact mechanical and thermal properties depend on the specific PLA brand and formulation. Request a material-specific specification when engineering performance matters.
