# ABS

## Availability

ELACE reports ABS as an available FDM filament type.

## Typical use

ABS may be considered for functional components where toughness and higher temperature resistance than general-purpose PLA are relevant.

## Important

Exact performance depends on the specific ABS formulation, print settings and part geometry. Confirm the material specification for critical applications.
