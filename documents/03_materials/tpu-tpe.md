# TPU and TPE

## Availability

ELACE reports both TPU and TPE flexible filament capability.

ELACE reports flexible TPU grades including 95A and 87A.

## Typical use

Flexible materials may be considered for:
- grips,
- flexible covers,
- compliant components,
- protective elements,
- flexible prototypes.

## Important

Hardness values such as 87A or 95A describe a material grade and do not by themselves define the complete mechanical behavior of a finished printed part.
