# PETG

## Availability

ELACE reports PETG as an available FDM filament type.

## Typical use

PETG can be considered when a balance of toughness, durability and ease of printing is required.

## Important

Exact properties vary by brand and formulation. Confirm the selected filament's datasheet when numerical engineering performance is required.
