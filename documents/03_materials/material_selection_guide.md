# Material Selection Guide

## Materials reported as available

ELACE reports broad FDM filament availability, including:
- PLA
- PETG
- ABS
- ASA
- TPE
- TPU
- flexible TPU grades including 95A and 87A
- specialty/silk filaments
- customer-requested brands/materials subject to availability

Common SLA resin categories include:
- standard resin,
- ABS-like resin,
- transparent resin,
- other requested resins subject to sourcing.

## General selection guidance

### PLA

Generally useful for:
- visual models,
- prototypes,
- general-purpose parts,
- decorative models.

### PETG

Generally useful where a balance of toughness and ease of printing is desired.

### ABS

Generally useful for functional applications where toughness and higher-temperature capability than PLA may be required.

### ASA

Generally useful for applications requiring weather/UV resistance compared with many general-purpose filaments.

### TPU/TPE

Flexible materials suitable for applications requiring elasticity or compliance.

### SLA standard resin

Useful for detailed models and visual prototypes where smooth finish and fine features are priorities.

### ABS-like resin

Useful when a resin with a more functional/tougher behavior than standard resin is desired, subject to the exact resin formulation.

### Transparent resin

Useful where visual transparency/translucency is desired, subject to the selected resin and post-processing.

## Important

Material properties vary significantly by brand and formulation. The chatbot must not provide a specific tensile strength, temperature rating or other numerical property unless the exact material/product datasheet is present in the knowledge base.

For critical applications, ask ELACE to confirm material suitability.
