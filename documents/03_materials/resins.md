# SLA Resins

## Regular resin

ELACE commonly uses standard/regular resin for detailed SLA printing.

## ABS-like resin

ABS-like resin is available for applications requiring a different balance of toughness and functionality than standard resin.

## Transparent resin

Transparent resin is available for suitable visual applications.

## Custom resins

Customers can request specific resin types. ELACE reports approximately 3–4 days for sourcing commonly obtainable requested resin and approximately 1–2 weeks for rare resin depending on availability.

## Important

Exact properties depend on the manufacturer and resin formulation. Numerical strength, temperature and optical properties should only be stated when the exact resin product is documented.
