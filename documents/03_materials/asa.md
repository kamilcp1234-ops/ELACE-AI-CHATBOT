# ASA

## Availability

ELACE reports ASA as an available FDM filament type.

## Typical use

ASA can be considered for applications where outdoor durability and UV/weather resistance are important.

## Important

Exact performance depends on the selected brand and formulation. Confirm material suitability for critical outdoor or engineering applications.
