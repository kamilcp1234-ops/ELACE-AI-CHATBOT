# ELACE 3D Printing Labs — Company Overview

## Company

ELACE 3D Printing Labs is a 3D printing company operating under the motto **Dream, Design and Develop**.

## What ELACE does

ELACE provides:
- FDM 3D printing
- SLA/MSLA 3D printing
- Prototyping
- 3D design
- Model creation from 2D references
- Custom model creation
- Enclosures and custom components
- Post-processing
- Painting and artistic finishing

## Customer types

ELACE serves:
- Businesses and industrial customers
- B2B customers
- B2C customers
- Students
- Customers with specific/custom use cases

ELACE also works with companies under NDAs for confidential designs.

## Custom engineering and design

Customers may approach ELACE with:
- an existing 3D model,
- an image,
- a 2D reference,
- or an idea/particular use case.

ELACE can provide 3D/CAD design assistance and can attempt to repair model issues where practical.

## Student support

For simple designs or small student requirements, design charges are generally waived. Student customers may also receive a discount depending on the order.

The standard design charge is approximately ₹500–₹600 per hour depending on design complexity, but this charge is generally waived for students for simple designs.

## Confidentiality

ELACE has signed NDAs with multiple companies for design-related work. Specific confidential customer information must not be disclosed through the chatbot.
