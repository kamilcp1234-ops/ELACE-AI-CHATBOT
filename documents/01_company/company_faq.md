# ELACE 3D Labs — Company FAQ

## What does ELACE 3D Labs do?

ELACE provides FDM and SLA/MSLA 3D printing, prototyping, 3D design, model creation, custom components, architectural models and post-processing.

## What is ELACE's motto?

Dream, Design and Develop.

## Do you work with businesses?

Yes. ELACE serves B2B and industrial customers as well as individual customers and students.

## Do you work with students?

Yes. ELACE supports student projects. Simple student designs or small requirements may receive waived design charges and student discounts.

## Can ELACE work on confidential designs?

Yes. ELACE has worked with companies under NDAs. Specific confidential project information should be handled directly with the authorized ELACE team rather than disclosed through a public chatbot.

## Can ELACE design a model from an idea?

ELACE provides 3D/CAD design assistance and can work from an idea, image or 2D reference, subject to feasibility.

## Can ELACE repair my 3D model?

ELACE can attempt to repair model issues where practical. Success depends on the geometry and nature of the problem.

## Can ELACE make large models?

Yes. Individual printer build volumes limit a single print, but larger models can be split into multiple sections and assembled. ELACE has produced a model approximately eight feet in overall size using multiple printed sections and joints.
