# ELACE 3D Labs — Services

## FDM 3D Printing

ELACE provides FDM 3D printing for functional parts, prototypes, models, architectural models, custom components and other requirements.

## SLA/MSLA 3D Printing

ELACE provides resin printing for smooth, detailed and feature-rich models where fine surface quality and small features are important.

## Prototyping

ELACE supports prototype creation for students, businesses and individual customers.

## 3D Design and CAD

Customers can approach ELACE with an image, idea or 2D reference. ELACE can create a corresponding 3D model subject to feasibility.

## 2D-to-3D Model Creation

ELACE can create 3D models from suitable 2D references or customer concepts.

## Model Repair

ELACE can attempt to repair customer-supplied 3D models that have geometry or printability issues. Repair is performed where practical and cannot be guaranteed for every model.

## Enclosures and Custom Components

ELACE can produce enclosures and other custom components for specific applications.

## Post-processing

Available post-processing includes:
- sanding,
- smoothing,
- matte painting,
- glossy painting,
- artistic painting for suitable models.

## Architectural Models

ELACE produces architectural models for presentations, design validation, concept visualization and display purposes. Models can be customized to project requirements.

## Batch/Bulk Orders

ELACE can handle bulk requirements. Pricing for bulk orders may be negotiable depending on order quantity and requirements.

## Student Orders

Students receive special consideration. Simple student designs may have design charges waived, and discounts may be available.

## Confidential Projects

ELACE can work on confidential company designs under applicable NDA arrangements.
