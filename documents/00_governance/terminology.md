# ELACE 3D Labs Terminology

## ELACE 3D Labs

Company name: ELACE 3D Printing Labs.

## Dream, Design and Develop

ELACE 3D Printing Labs' motto is:

**Dream, Design and Develop.**

## FDM

FDM means Fused Deposition Modeling. ELACE uses FDM printers for functional parts, prototypes, general-purpose models and other applications according to customer requirements.

## SLA

ELACE uses resin-based MSLA/SLA printing for smooth, high-detail and feature-rich parts and models.

## CAD

CAD means computer-aided design. ELACE provides computer-aided 3D design assistance based on customer requirements, images or ideas.

## STL

STL is a common 3D-printing model file format. When submitting an STL, the customer should clearly specify the intended exact dimensions because STL scaling can sometimes cause the model to be interpreted at an unintended size.

## STEP

STEP is the preferred file format for model submission when available.

## OBJ

OBJ files are accepted.

## Blender files

Blender files can also be accepted where practical.

## Post-processing

ELACE offers sanding/smoothing and painting. Artistic painting can also be provided for suitable models.

## Custom print

A custom print is a print produced according to a customer's specific requirement, design or supplied model.

## Lead time

Lead time refers to the time required for production and/or preparation before shipment. Actual timing depends on print size, quantity, printer availability, material availability and order readiness.
